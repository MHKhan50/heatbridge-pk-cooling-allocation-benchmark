# Pakistan context sources

These sources explain why the problem matters. They are not inputs to the generator and do not validate its parameters.

1. Urban Unit, Government of Punjab (2025). *Heatwave Management Plan of Lahore (2025-2028)*. https://urbanunit.gov.pk/Download/publications/Files/8/2025/Heatwave%20Management%20Plan%20of%20Lahore_06_2025%5B1%5D.pdf
2. Latif, B., Ahsaan, F. and Latif, A. (2025). Heatwaves and water scarcity in Pakistan: a climate-health emergency demanding global attention. *BMJ Global Health*, 10(12), e020980. https://gh.bmj.com/content/10/12/e020980
3. World Health Organization. *Climate change and heat and health* (fact sheet). https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health

## Data availability

No census, shelter, outage, water-service or heat-health data is bundled; everything is synthetic. A real-city study would need licensed geospatial, census, utility and facility data and accessibility assumptions checked with local stakeholders.
