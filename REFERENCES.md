# Pakistan context and reference sources

These sources establish the relevance of heat preparedness and compound service disruption in Pakistan. They are not inputs to the synthetic generator and do not validate its parameter distributions.

1. National Disaster Management Authority (Pakistan). *Heat Wave Survival Guide and Best Practices* (2025). https://ndma.gov.pk/storage/publications/September2025/H0TRwZsZOaolKfv6rhdi.pdf
2. Urban Unit, Government of Punjab. *Heatwave Management Plan of Lahore (2025–2028)* (2025). https://urbanunit.gov.pk/Download/publications/Files/8/2025/Heatwave%20Management%20Plan%20of%20Lahore_06_2025%5B1%5D.pdf
3. Latif, B., Ahsaan, F., & Latif, A. (2025). Heatwaves and water scarcity in Pakistan: a climate-health emergency demanding global attention. *BMJ Global Health*, 10, e020980. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjgh-2025-020980
4. World Health Organization. *Heat and health* (fact sheet). https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health
5. UNICEF Innocenti (2025). *Thermal risks: extreme heat, oppressive cold* (Pakistan report). https://www.unicef.org/pakistan/media/7566/file/UNICEF-Innocenti-GPR-Pakistan-Report-2025.pdf.pdf

## Data availability statement

No city-level census, shelter inventory, power-outage, water-service, or heat-health dataset is bundled. This release uses synthetic data only. Before a real-city deployment or empirical paper, obtain appropriately licensed and documented geospatial, census, utility, facility, and stakeholder-validated accessibility data.
